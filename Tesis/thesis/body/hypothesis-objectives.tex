\section{Hypothesis}
\label{section:hypothesis}

The studies reviewed in Chapter 2 demonstrate that sound sources can be located in three-dimensional space with some precision, rooms can be recognized by their acoustic signatures, depth images of parts of a room can be created using only audible sound echoes, and object shapes can be inferred from acoustic features extracted from echoes originating inside the objects. Collectively, these findings suggest that a function can be learned to map the room impulse response (RIR) to the location of an object in an enclosed space. Based on these findings, our hypothesis is:

"An algorithm pipeline with beamforming and ESN that utilize the rich RIR spatial-geometrical information comprehended in a set of acoustic signals can infer the location and orientation of a cube of 20cm side allocated inside an small enclosed space of near of 1\(m^3\) \footnote{Region with acoustic superposition found by \cite{bat1}}, this with a movement distance resolution of at least 5cm and achieve less than 2cm of Mean Absolute Error in position and 10 degrees in orientation\footnote{Based on the size of the objects and maximum deformation presented in \cite{echosense}} with an error less than 6 degrees of Mean Absolute Error in the estimation from two consecutive positions."

\section{Objectives}
\label{section:objectives}
The current research established the following objectives:

\subsection{Main Objective}
\label{section:main-objective}
\label{objective:main}

To model and evaluate a processing pipeline that combines beamforming with CNN and ESN models to map multichannel acoustic signals to an object's spatial coordinates and orientation within an enclosed space.

\subsection{Specific Objectives}
\label{section:specific-objectives}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Collect a dataset of acoustic responses from an object at different locations in a room, together with the corresponding spatial coordinates and orientations.
	
	\item Develop a CNN-based reference method that learns to estimate an object's coordinates and orientation from the dataset.

    \item Develop a resource-efficient ESN-based method that learns to estimate an object's coordinates and orientation from the dataset.

    \item Build a beamforming stage that divides the measurement space into four regions based on acoustic source locations.
	
	\item Experimentally evaluate the accuracy of the estimators.
	
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Deliverables}
The following deliverables are associated with each specific objective defined for the thesis:

\textbf{Specific Objective 1:}
 A dataset containing acoustic recordings of echoes in the form of time series and its corresponding object location-orientation.

\textbf{Specific Objective 2:}
Thesis document with detailed description of the concepts, observation and results analysis that justify the system design with CNN.

\textbf{Specific Objective 3:}
Thesis document with detailed description of the concepts, observation and results analysis that justify the system design with ESN.

\textbf{Specific Objective 4:}
Thesis document with detailed description of the concepts, observation and results analysis that justify the beamforming.

\textbf{Specific Objective 5:}
Thesis document with analysis and evaluation of the proposed solutions considering performance metrics under experimental operation.  

\section{Scope and Limitations}

%Should include the scope and limitations of the developed research.

\textbf{Scope and limitation 1:} 
The development of the measure box and the collection of the dataset is part of the work. 

\textbf{Scope and limitation 2:}
Only two ML approaches are considered: CNN as the ground truth and ESN as an efficient solution.    

\textbf{Scope and limitation 3:} 
The proposed pipelines will not work on real time, even for the experimental evaluation the data will be first collected and later presented to the model in order to obtain the evaluation metrics.

\textbf{Scope and limitation 4:} 
The programming language is python, the hardware to process the data will be a desktop personal computer.
